title: circe: herbs, empowerment, and ancient greece
blurb: Inspired by Madeline Miller’s novel, *Circe*

she quells the herbs that tremble
beneath her toes trodden in musky soil
at sunrise the mandrake families whisper their cloying pollen
into the swaying breeze
roots begging to be useful after centuries of wintry shadow
plucked dry by fingertips thirsting for darkness
she dries her herbs
all eager to extract
what power surges through the roots of the underground

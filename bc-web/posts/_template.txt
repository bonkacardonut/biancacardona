title: my new post title

Copy this file, rename it like 04-my-new-post.txt, and replace this text.
(Files starting with _ are skipped, so this template never shows up on the site.)

The part after the number becomes the URL: 04-my-new-post.txt -> /blog/my-new-post
The number sets the order on the blog page.

How text works:
- A blank line starts a new paragraph.
- A single line break stays a line break (good for poems).
- Wrap words in *stars* for italics.
- Lines starting with "> " become a quote. A quote line starting with "> ~ " is the attribution.

Then run:  python3 build.py

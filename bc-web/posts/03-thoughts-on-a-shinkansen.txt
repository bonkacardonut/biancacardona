title: thoughts on a *shinkansen*

I’m on the shinkansen from Ginza to Kyoto, neck craning a near ninety degrees. A thick fog shrouds the emerald forest beyond my reflection in the window, fading in and out, pulsing dark and bright, like my streams of consciousness. In this seat, I shut myself off to the entirety of the world but my croissant, C100 and earphones. The journey ahead is outstretched; so is my peace.

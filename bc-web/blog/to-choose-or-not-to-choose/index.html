to choose or not to choose

choice paralysis: in a sea of opportunities, focusing on one fish feels paralyzing—

because how could you choose between paths if you don’t know how they end? there is no way to determine whether a risk or the safe route pays off, or if you should optimize your life for stability before even attempting creative pursuits. a door opens only to lock you out from the inside. restrained from tracing back our steps, it feels as though there is no way to undo the decisions we make about what we want to contribute to the world. we want to optimize our paths, our work, our relationships, our interests, all hoping that the path that meets our pattering feet is the right one.

except life is not meant to be optimized. choices always come wrapped in risk. the fact that we can choose what we want for ourselves, though it’s an uncomfortable amount of freedom at times, is what separates us from other mammals. there is beauty in not knowing where our choices will take us. no path is inherently right or wrong, just aligned or astray from the pillars of our lives. behind each “failed” path blooms a flower bud that marks our growth and the seeds we carry into our next choice.

i’m still a little convinced that the uncertainty i felt when discussing what i’d love to do in the future is some irreparable crime. it’s as though everyone has already chosen a door, and i’m the only one scrambling through all of the options. i can’t see the end of the road—

but i’ll keep soaking in the world around me on this ride.
